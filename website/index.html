The words and cache state here are illustrative. Actual token equality is established by the benchmark above.

01

Correctness first

Commit only the matching greedy prefix and the target correction. Tokenizer mapping handles the Large-v3 / Tiny vocabulary difference.

02

Persistent cache, bounded buffers

Reuse encoder cross-attention state. Roll back self-attention KV state after a mismatch, with buffered tokens and device-side prefix decisions.

03

Profile, then simplify

Cached suppression masks remove repeated isin and unique work. Fixed k = 4 remains the default after comparison with adaptive drafting.

Less repeated launch work.
